Welcome to Amazon Redirect Plus

This extension helps you quickly switch Amazon storefronts (US, UK, France, etc.) while keeping the same product page.

🌍 Current Domain Mode

If you leave your Home Store as Current Domain, you will see a dropdown on product pages that lets you jump between storefronts.

🏠 Home Store Mode

If you choose a Home Store (like 🇬🇧 UK), product pages will show a Go to button that instantly redirects you to that store.

🔎 Out of Stock Search Button

When an item is out of stock, the extension can show a button to search for the model number using your preferred search engine.
